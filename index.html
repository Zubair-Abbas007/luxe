Our Philosophy

Fashion That
Means Something

LUXE was founded in New York in 2014 by designer Isabelle Marceau with a single conviction: that true luxury is not about price — it is about intention.

Every garment we produce is ethically made in family-run ateliers across Milan, Lisbon, and Seoul. We use certified organic cotton, recycled cashmere, and deadstock silk.

We don't do fast fashion. We do forever fashion.
